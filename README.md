Understanding Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, particularly computer systems. These processes include learning (the acquisition of information and rules for using it), reasoning (the use of rules to reach approximate or definite conclusions), and self-correction. With the advent of AI, numerous industries have experienced transformative changes, leading to heightened efficiency and innovative solutions to age-old problems.
